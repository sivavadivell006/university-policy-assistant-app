TITLE: Examination Instructions for Candidates
DOC_ID: EXM-2023-04
ISSUED: 2023-09-01
CATEGORY: examination

SECTION 1: Reporting time and hall rules
Be at your seat 20 minutes before the exam starts. You may enter up to 30 minutes after the start only with the Chief Superintendent's permission. You may not leave the hall during the first 60 minutes.

SECTION 2: Documents you must carry
Carry your college ID card and your hall ticket to every exam. You will not be allowed to write without a hall ticket. If you lost it, collect a duplicate from the Controller of Examinations office (fee Rs. 200) before the exam.

SECTION 3: Items not allowed in the exam hall
Mobile phones, smart watches, earphones, programmable calculators and any written or printed material are not allowed, even if a phone is switched off. Leave such items at the designated counter at your own risk, or do not bring them.

SECTION 4: Malpractice
Having unauthorised material or a mobile phone at your seat is treated as malpractice and is referred to the Examination Malpractice Committee. Depending on severity, the penalty ranges from cancellation of that paper to cancellation of all papers of the semester.
