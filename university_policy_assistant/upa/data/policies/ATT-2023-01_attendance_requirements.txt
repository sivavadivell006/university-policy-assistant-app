TITLE: Attendance Requirements Policy
DOC_ID: ATT-2023-01
ISSUED: 2023-07-10
CATEGORY: attendance

SECTION 1: Minimum attendance to write end-semester exams
You need at least 75% attendance in each course to be allowed to write the end-semester examination for that course. Theory and lab courses are counted separately. Attendance is counted from the first working day of the semester to the last instructional day, as recorded by the course faculty on the attendance portal.

SECTION 2: On-duty attendance
Days on which you officially represent the college (inter-college sports, technical symposiums, NCC/NSS camps) are counted as present. The on-duty letter signed by your HoD must be given to your class advisor within 5 working days of returning. Letters submitted later are not accepted.

SECTION 3: Condonation of attendance shortage
If your attendance in a course is between 65% and 74%, you may apply for condonation. You must (1) submit a condonation application to the HoD through your class advisor, (2) attach a medical certificate or other documentary proof for the absences, and (3) pay a condonation fee of Rs. 300 per course. Attendance below 65% cannot be condoned and you will be detained in that course.

SECTION 4: Students detained for shortage of attendance
A student detained for shortage of attendance must re-register for the course in its next regular offering and attend it afresh. Marks from the earlier attempt are not carried forward.
