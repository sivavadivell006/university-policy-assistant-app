TITLE: Re-evaluation and Photocopy Procedure
DOC_ID: REV-2023-07
ISSUED: 2023-09-15
CATEGORY: reevaluation

SECTION 1: Who can apply for re-evaluation
Re-evaluation is available for theory papers only. Lab, practical and project viva results cannot be re-evaluated. You must have appeared for the exam.

SECTION 2: Time window and fee
Apply within 5 working days of the date the results are published. The fee is Rs. 1000 per paper. A photocopy of your answer script costs Rs. 300 per paper.

SECTION 3: Steps to request re-evaluation
1. Optionally request a photocopy of your answer script using Form PC-02 to see how your paper was marked.
2. Fill in Re-evaluation Form RE-01 with your register number, course code and exam session.
3. Pay the re-evaluation fee for each paper and keep the receipt.
4. Submit Form RE-01 and the fee receipt at the Controller of Examinations office before the window closes.
5. Re-evaluation results are announced within 15 working days.

SECTION 4: Outcome of re-evaluation
The higher of the original and the re-evaluated marks is retained. There is no second re-evaluation; further grievances go to the Examination Grievance Committee.
