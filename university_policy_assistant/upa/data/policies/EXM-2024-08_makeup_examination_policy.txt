TITLE: Make-up Examination Policy
DOC_ID: EXM-2024-08
ISSUED: 2024-01-15
CATEGORY: examination

SECTION 1: Who is eligible for a make-up examination
You may apply for a make-up examination if you missed an end-semester exam because of hospitalisation (supported by a medical certificate and hospital record), the death of an immediate family member, or representing the university at a national-level event. Timetable clashes, oversleeping and travel delays are not valid reasons.

SECTION 2: How to apply for a make-up examination
Apply within 3 working days of the missed exam by submitting Form EX-07 with supporting documents to the Controller of Examinations office. The fee is Rs. 1000 per paper. The make-up exam is held within 30 days of the regular exam.
